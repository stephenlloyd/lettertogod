Is the app free?

Yes — completely free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start praying.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Berean Standard Bible is bundled in the app, so reading and prayer work with no connection — on a plane, on the train, anywhere.

Where are my prayers stored?

On your device only. They never leave your phone, and we cannot read them.

Something isn’t working.

Email us with your device model and a short description and we’ll help as fast as we can.
