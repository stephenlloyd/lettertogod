What happens next

  1. Get the app and open it.
  2. Enter the code above when it asks whether you were invited.
  3. Read a chapter whenever you like. Answer one question about it, and the chapter is recorded.
  4. Give what you read to your church, if you go to one.

Being straightforward. An invitation earns nobody anything — not the person who sent it, and not you. There is no money in this and there is nothing to sell you beyond the app itself. It is a way of reading scripture alongside somebody, and that is all it is.
