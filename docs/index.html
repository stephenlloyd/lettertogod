What it does

A quiet space to pray

📜

Guided prayer

Compose a personal prayer step by step — adoration, thanksgiving, and asking — with gentle prompts, or write freely in your own words.

✟

Verse discovery

Discover Bible verses that meet the moment. The full Berean Standard Bible is built in and works completely offline.

🔒

Private archive

Keep a personal record of your prayers. Everything stays on your device — no account, no cloud, no one else can read it.

Reading together

Give what you read to your church

Finish a chapter, answer one question about it, and the chapter is recorded. Whenever you like, give everything you have read to your church, so the reading counts for the people you sit with on Sunday.
